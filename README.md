# InovaHealth AI Clinical Decision Support

AI powered clinical decision support that transforms fragmented health records into longitudinal insights, connects patient data with international evidence based guidelines, and helps clinicians identify trends, strengthen diagnostic reasoning, and personalize care.

## The Problem

Clinical information is often fragmented across laboratory reports, imaging studies, prescriptions, medical history, and consultation notes. This makes it difficult to understand the complete patient trajectory and recognize meaningful changes over time.

## The Solution

InovaHealth provides a clinical reasoning layer between raw health data and medical decision making. It organizes longitudinal records, identifies relevant trends, interprets findings in context, and connects clinical information with evidence based guidelines.

The platform supports clinicians without replacing professional judgment.

## Key Capabilities

- Longitudinal organization of patient health data
- Integrated interpretation of laboratory findings
- Identification of clinically relevant trends
- Guideline criteria applied to the clinical context
- Structured comparison of therapeutic options
- Personalized monitoring and follow up priorities
- Visible uncertainty and source traceability
- Human oversight for every clinical decision

## How We Used Codex and GPT-5.6

InovaHealth was developed from concept to implementation using OpenAI Codex with GPT-5.6 as the primary engineering and reasoning environment.

Codex and GPT-5.6 helped us:

- Translate real clinical workflows into product requirements and software architecture
- Build the responsive interface with Astro, TypeScript, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS
- Structure longitudinal patient records and clinical reasoning workflows
- Develop reusable components for laboratory interpretation, guideline mapping, and personalized follow up
- Debug the application and continuously refine usability and visual quality
- Organize scientific references and connect clinical criteria with international guidelines
- Identify uncertainty, missing information, and safety boundaries in clinical outputs
- Create synthetic patient scenarios for privacy safe demonstrations
- Produce technical documentation, project media, and the hackathon demo

GPT-5.6 enabled medicine, software engineering, scientific evidence, and product design to be developed within the same workflow.

Every clinical output remains subject to professional review. Codex and GPT-5.6 support clinical reasoning but do not replace medical judgment.

## Clinical Safety

InovaHealth is designed as clinical decision support, not as an autonomous diagnostic or treatment system. Outputs are prepared for professional review and include clinical context, uncertainty, evidence, and clear limitations.

All public demonstrations use synthetic patient data.
